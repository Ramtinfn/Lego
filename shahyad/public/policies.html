Privacy

We collect only what an order needs: your name, delivery address, email and phone number, collected and processed by Stripe when you pay. We use these to fulfil and deliver your order and to contact you about it. If you email us to be notified about availability, we use your address once for that purpose. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site, we do not sell or share your details with anyone except the carriers who deliver your parcel, and we keep order records only as long as tax law requires. To access or delete your details, email ramtinf@gmail.com. Payment details never reach us — they are handled entirely by Stripe (Stripe's privacy policy).
